🔒 How LifeBuddy Understands Your Routine

LifeBuddy is 100% private. All predictions and insights are computed locally for your account. Here is how your activity is used:

  • Focus Sessions: Identifies your peak energy hours with Bayesian smoothing.
  • Tasks: Learns actual duration history to predict future task estimates.
  • Habits: Tracks streak compounding to alert you before you break a rhythm.
  • Finances: Computes daily spending burn rate to project month-end budgets.
  • Daily Reflections: Discovers correlations between rest and focus energy.
🛡️ Zero third-party trackers, zero ad cookies, and zero external telemetry.
